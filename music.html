Four independent interaction concepts for the Spotify project section. Switch tabs to compare each one on its own — only one or two are meant to survive.

Project

01 · Interactive Vinyl Record

Idles in a slow spin, speeds up on hover, and can be grabbed with a mouse or finger and spun by hand. Let go and it keeps turning with real momentum before settling back into its idle spin. The center label opens the project.

Drag + momentumPointer eventsKeyboard nudge

Fits: music.html hero, which currently has no hero visual — center label would link to the live Spotify profile.

02 · Audio Waveform / Equalizer

A minimal bar visualizer that breathes gently at rest and turns energetic on hover, focus, or when it scrolls into view — meant to sit quietly rather than compete for attention.

Transform-only barsHover / in-view triggerAmbient

Fits: drop-in replacement for the .badge-bars icon on the project cards (index.html:136–147, used at 443/473/502), and reusable as music.html's now-playing pulse.
