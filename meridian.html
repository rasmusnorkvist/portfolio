Business Interface

A browser-based prototype of a personal AI workspace, styled after native macOS apps like Raycast and Notion: a morning briefing, inbox, notes, a task board, voice capture, and a calendar, all pulled together by a command-palette AI assistant.

Everything below is fully mocked, there's no real inbox, no real model, no network calls. It's a fast way to validate the workflow and the feel before wiring up anything real.
